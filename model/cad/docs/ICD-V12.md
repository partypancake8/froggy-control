The wheel STEP is manufacturer design evidence, not certification that every generic Ender wheel matches. The two bearing inner faces must seat on the 1 mm internal shim without locking the wheel. Finite tread width produces differential sliding on a rotating disc: inspect heat/wear after low-speed unloaded testing. Motor pulley details, load ratings, frame profile compatibility, belt tension, actual tolerances, print strength, and assembled runout remain physical qualification items.
