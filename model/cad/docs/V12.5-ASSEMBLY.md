Testing build caveats:

- Plate A has only a 1.16 mm web between each 4.3 pillow-block hole and the 16 mm centre hole. That is about three extrusion lines and the first place to crack. Light torque, and inspect after the first runs. **No washers under the four pillow-block screw heads on either face** (fix A, I033): a 9 OD washer under a head reaches r 6.81 and lifts the block; the heads must stay clear of the spinning race shims.
- Keep M4 screws into or through PETG to about 0.5 to 1 N m (9 mm OD washers only where no rotating part is near; never under the pillow-block heads). This is a testing-build limit for plastic, not an approved fastener torque.
- A NEMA 17 case can run near PETG's softening point (glass transition about 80 C). Check case temperature on the bench.
- PETG creeps faster when warm: re-tension the belt after a warm run, and recheck screw preload after the first spin-ups.
- PETG is about 1/30 as stiff as 6061, so the bearing can tilt more under rotor imbalance. Treat any spin data from the printed plates as testing data, not qualification of the aluminium build.

## Revision and load path

V12 keeps the V11 pillow block, Hyper Hub, 72 mm REX shaft, two 20T pulleys, 100 mm belt and 17HS19 motor arrangement. The former 8 mm-bore polyurethane rim wheels are replaced by standard Ender POM wheel assemblies on horizontal M5 axles. Their **flat outer treads** contact the underside of the disc. The V shoulders and bearing side faces do not support the disc.

Three supports are revised for the larger, wider wheels and 5.4 mm axle slots. Their original plate-A mounting holes remain. Stock 3 mm metal spacers now support plate A and the corner clamp tabs directly on the 2020; the acrylic is cut away from the entire underside footprint of these plates. This removes acrylic from those clamp paths without moving the spindle datums. Ten drive mounting nuts now have the specified 5 mm DIN985 envelopes, replacing the earlier shorter plain-nut representations.

Normal gravity/axial load path: carrier -> Hyper Hub -> upper bearing inner race -> bearing/housing -> plate A -> four metal hardpoints -> cross rails -> bracketed deck -> legs. Belt torque path: motor D shaft -> 5 mm pulley -> belt -> REX pulley -> REX shaft -> Hyper Hub -> carrier. The hub (top) and the REX pulley set screws through the race spacer and shims (bottom) locate the shaft axially; end screws/washer stacks provide backup retention. Rim supports are set to light stabilizing contact. Neither plastic support creep nor the pillow block's axial capacity has been qualified.

## Wheel supports and axle stack

Print three identical R02 supports (V12.5 gusset A, I038; `bin/cad printcheck fp9_roller_block`) in PETG **on their back**: the outboard wall's r 99.5 face on the bed (one planar 1155 mm2 face), layers stacking along r, no supports; only the two M4 holes bridge (Bambu P2S PETG, 3 walls, 25 % infill: 10.0 g, 38 min, 0 warnings). The V12 side guards are replaced by two triangular gussets with R 5 base coves, R 3 vertical corners and R 5 coves onto the wall; slot, holes and wall top unchanged. A starting process is 0.2 mm layers, at least six perimeters and locally solid infill around the axle wall and mounting holes. These are fabrication starting settings, not a certified print-strength specification. Inspect layer bonding; remove slot bridges/strings without enlarging the wall or rounding its clamping faces.

Support mounting uses two M4x 16 socket screws and M4 DIN985 nuts per support, through 4 mm support base plus 6 mm plate A. The nuts are 5 mm high; nominal screw protrusion is 1 mm. Install these on the bench with plate A accessible underneath.

From **outside toward the center**, each axle is:

1. M5x 25 socket-screw head, outside the support wall.
2. Support wall, 4 mm, with 5.4 mm vertical slot.
3. External precision washer, 5 ID/10 OD/1 mm.
4. Assembled wheel, 11 mm between outer bearing faces: 625 bearing 5 mm + internal precision washer 1 mm +625 bearing 5 mm. POM shell surrounds this stack.
5. Second external 5/10/1 precision washer.
6. M5 DIN985 nut, 5 mm high, inboard. Nylon end faces the projecting screw tip.

Grip before nut is 17 mm; total stack through nut 22 mm, leaving 3 mm screw protrusion. The two external washers load the bearing inner races. Their overhang must not rub the recessed shields on the actual supplied bearings. The internal washer prevents the two inner races being squeezed together when the nut is tightened. Tighten while holding the wheel height and verify the wheel still spins freely. Binding is a stack/fit fault to correct, not something to overcome by leaving a loose axle bolt.

Wheel axle center is radius 89 and Z32.95; top of 24.1 OD tread is Z45, equal to the carrier underside. The flat tread spans radius 85.95..92.05, leaving 0.95 mm to the carrier edge. The stock model's plastic width is 10.2; bearing faces span 11. The manufacturer's current product description says 24 x 10.24, so generic replacements must be measured. Geometry matches the archived official CAD, not every seller's use of the word "standard."

Available axle-center travel is Z30.95..34.95. Remove the carrier to use the full range: raising above nominal intentionally intersects an installed disc. Install the bare carrier, raise each wheel to just contact using equal light paper drag, and lock the nut without changing height. Turn by hand through 360 degrees and repeat. Do not jack the rotor up with the wheels or use three hard preloads to hide a tilted spindle. A finite-width flat wheel has differential surface speed across a rotating disc; inspect for POM heat, dust and track wear during initial low-speed unloaded testing.

A straight 8 mm nut socket with OD no more than 13 mm has the modeled inboard approach. A 4 mm hex driver has the outboard approach with the cage removed. This checks the straight tool envelope, not the swing of every ratchet or L key. Set the supports before reinstalling the cage.

## Spindle and motor assembly sequence

1. On the bench, attach D02 pillow block to P01 with four M4x 12 ISO 7380 button screws upward from below, **no washers** on either face. Nominal engagement is 6 mm. The heads sit at Z 0.8..3.0 on r 11.31; the nearest spinning part (the lowest race shim, r 5.5) is 2.01 mm away (BRepExtrema, swept 360 deg), and the REX pulley hub top (Z -0.5) is below them. Keep the block's bearing outer rings and housing seated; do not substitute generic 8 mm hex bearings for the REX profile.
2. Attach all three wheel supports and the Hall bracket while P01's underside remains accessible. The Hall bracket uses two M3x 10 button screws into plate A; its board uses one M3x 6 screw into a heat-set insert and rests against the modeled ledge. Keep cables clear of the future belt/shaft path; wiring is not yet modeled.
3. Pop the factory e-clip off the 72 mm REX shaft (keep it as a spare); its groove end goes down. Insert the shaft through the pillow block. Shaft ends are Z -24.8 and +47.2 (0.2 proud of the hub pilot, step 5). From below, slide on in order: the 1522 race spacer (8 ID x 10 OD x 6), up through plate A's 16 hole onto the lower bearing inner race (Z 3..9, 3.0 radial clearance to the hole); then the 2807 shims (8 x 11 x 1), nominal 3 (Z 0..3).
3a. Loop the GT2 belt over the shaft below plate A BEFORE the REX pulley is slid on; the belt cannot pass over a pulley flange at any slot position (minimum centre distance for that is 26.6, the slot stops at 28.3).
3b. Then slide on the REX pulley through the belt loop, hub up, slid up to the lowest shim with its set screws only snug for now (final Z -18..0, groove centre Z -13.0). Shim rule: measure the hub, block, pulley, spacer and shims, then choose the count so the pulley hub top stays at least 1.0 below the block screw heads and the lower backup spacer still fits (step 4). Do not transmit axial clamp loads through pulley flanges.
4. On the lower shaft end install the 1522 backup spacer (Z -24.8..-18.8; it covers the e-clip groove at Z -21.6..-20.8, so the factory e-clip must be off), the 12 mm OD washer (Z -25.8..-24.8) and the M4x 8 end screw. The washer seats on the shaft end; the spacer rests on it, 0.8 below the pulley, and is never clamped (clamping it against the pulley would pull the race column into the bearings). Measure actual tapped depth and verify the screw seats before bottoming.
5. Fit the Hyper Hub, lower boss against the upper bearing inner race, shaft end 0.1 to 0.3 proud of the hub pilot (nominal 0.2, Z 47.2): never flush or below, or the top washer lands on the hub and the end screw preloads the bearings. Tighten the two M4 pinch bolts (3 mm key) on the bench. Then set the pulley stop, bench only: push the REX pulley up so the shims, race spacer and lower inner race seat without free axial play and without preload, and tighten its two M5 set screws (2.5 key from the side, axis Z -5.0, 8.0 below plate A's underside). These set screws are the lower axial stop; once the cartridge is in the deck the 2020 rails block every key approach at that plane, so the stop can only be set or re-set on the bench. Turn by hand. Add the top 12 mm washer (Z 47.2..48.2) and M4x 8 end screw; it must seat on the shaft end only. The top head reaches about Z 50.4, below carrier top Z 51, inside its 14.3 center opening.
5a. Bench note: after the pulley is set, feeler-check the gap between the backup 1522 spacer and the pulley bottom; if under 0.3 mm replace the backup 1522 with five 2807 shims.
6. Fit the motor up into the slab's bottom pocket turned 180 degrees about its axis, so its lead glands face +Y (out of the open pocket end), away from the spindle's lower end screw; four M3x 10 socket screws and 0.5 mm M3 washers from the slab top through the 5.5 roof, initially loose in the slots. Motor face Z -25.5 (V12.5: 2.5 lower than V12). Thread engagement 4.0 mm versus the specified 4.5 mm tap depth (x 12 would pass it); check actual holes before tightening. Mount the 5 mm pulley directly on the D shaft with hub upward and groove center Z -13.0; its top (Z -1.5) is flush with the shaft tip. ## Belt alignment, tension and retained clearances

The drive is 1:1 with 20 teeth each at 2 mm pitch. Pitch radius 6.366 mm, nominal center distance 30 mm, belt pitch length 100 mm. Center travel is 28.5..30.5. At minimum center the pitch path is 97 mm, providing 3 mm nominal slack for installation. At maximum it is 101 mm; this is travel allowance, **not permission to stretch the belt 1 percent**. No arbitrary guitar-string or thumb-deflection criterion is assigned.

Set both groove center planes to Z -13.0 (fix A). Check with a straightedge or measured flange offsets; different pulley flange/overall heights are not identical reference faces. Obtain the belt supplier's working-tension and tension-setting method. Measure/set its specified pretension and record it, then lock the motor screws and recheck. If a frequency method is supplied, it requires the actual belt mass per length and free span, not a guessed number.

The unchanged V11 motor travel audit checked 21 positions and bounded intermediate noncontact separation at 1.2 mm. V12 repeats the travel check against the changed geometry. The intentional sliding motor-plate interface and fixed-shape belt are treated separately. Belt strain/tooth contact under rotation is not a flexible-body simulation.

At 0.2625 N m, belt tension difference is 41.2 N. With 11 mm spindle-bearing span and belt 24.25 mm below the lower bearing center, minimum reactions are approximately 132 N at the lower bearing and 91 N at the upper, before added pretension/external loads. At 0.52 N m they rise to 262/180 N. These are load demands, not allowable ratings. Axial bearing capacity and the motor's radial limit must be checked independently. The short belt solves transmission geometry; it does not establish bearing life.

## Validation evidence and physical acceptance

The validation JSON records full-model solid/contact checks, actual joint results, wheel internal-component checks, wheel adjustment poses, continuous flat-track containment, axle tool envelopes, deck insertion bounds, metal hardpoint contacts, and differences from V11. The first source-based wheel internal check found no overlaps among its 30 bodies. Full assembly rotation uses discrete poses; the unchanged planar annular track and axisymmetric tread provide continuous contact geometry only for that specific interface.

Before cutting final plates: verify purchased wheel OD/width/flat tread/bore, motor shaft/D-flat/body length, motor-pulley hub/tooth/grub dimensions, 2020 profile and nut/casting compatibility. Measure all spacers. Check the supplied screw lengths, thread depths, grades and supplier torque instructions. CAD nominal fit is insufficient to claim actual off-the-shelf fit when the detailed vendor drawing is absent.

Before powered loaded operation: establish pillow-block radial/axial ratings and motor radial rating; specify/measure belt pretension; confirm hub/collar/pulley screw torque and retention; indicator-check endplay and radial/axial runout; verify free rotation and no rubbing throughout360 degrees; confirm wheel bolts remain clamped without bearing drag; inspect support creep and frame racking; validate guarding, balance, wiring and controlled stopping. Record observations in `acceptance-record.csv`. Items without evidence remain OPEN, not PASS. No full-machine operating release is issued by this packet.
